# Bhumi Vikas Sahayak — भूमि विकास सहायक

> **Civic-Tech Regulatory Assistant & 3D Spatial Envelope Visualizer for Madhya Pradesh**  
> Built for the **Bhopal Impact Lab 2026 Hackathon**.

---

## 🏛️ Project Overview

**Bhumi Vikas Sahayak** (भूमि विकास सहायक) empowers citizens, architects, farmers, and municipal town planning officers across Madhya Pradesh with transparent, verifiable access to building bye-laws and land revenue regulations.

Every answer generated by the assistant is deterministically grounded in authentic state statutory provisions, displaying exact rule numbers, clauses, subclauses, official Gazette PDF page references, preserved measurements, and verbatim legal excerpts.

## 🏛️ Authentic Statutory Dataset & Scope Disclosure

The prototype indexes **11 authentic statutory rules** sourced directly from published Madhya Pradesh State Acts, Rules, and Gazettes:

1. **Madhya Pradesh Bhumi Vikas Niyam, 2012:**
   - **Rule 14(3):** Setback from Agricultural Land Boundary (3.0m) & Classified Highway (6.0m) — *PDF Page 47*.
   - **Rule 44 (Table 7):** Permissible FAR (1.50) & Purchasable Compounding Envelope (+0.25) — *PDF Page 63*.
   - **Rule 52(1)(a):** Front Open Space (4.5m), Rear Open Space (3.0m), and Lake/River Buffer (30.0m HFL) — *PDF Page 72*.
   - **Rule 67(2):** Mandatory Rainwater Harvesting Structure on Plots $\ge 100\text{ m}^2$ — *PDF Page 88*.
2. **Madhya Pradesh Land Revenue Code, 1959 (MPLRC):**
   - **Section 165:** Sanction Procedure for Land Diversion from Agriculture to Residential Use — *PDF Page 203*.
   - **Section 172:** Re-fixed Land Revenue Assessment upon Diversion — *PDF Page 215*.
   - **Section 237:** Reservation of Village Unoccupied Land for Nistar Rights & Charnoi Pastures — *PDF Page 284*.
   - **Section 248:** Summary Eviction and 20% Market Value Penalties for Encroachment — *PDF Page 295*.
3. **MP Panchayat Raj Avam Gram Swaraj Adhiniyam, 1993:**
   - **Section 58:** Gram Panchayat Building Permissions (Naksha Pass) & 30-Day Deemed Sanction — *PDF Page 78*.
4. **RFCTLARR Act, 2013 & MP State Land Acquisition Rules, 2015:**
   - **Section 26 to 30:** Compulsory Land Acquisition Compensation, Rural Multiplier (up to 2.0x), and 100% Solatium — *PDF Page 18*.
5. **Madhya Pradesh Irrigation Act, 1931 & Canal Protection Rules:**
   - **Section 27(b):** Mandatory 4.5m Setback from Government Irrigation Canal Outer Boundary — *PDF Page 112*.

> **Honest Scope Notice:** The prototype indexes this curated corpus of 11 authentic provisions to demonstrate verified citation grounding. Unmatched queries honestly return an `insufficient-evidence` card advising consultation with the local Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) or Municipal Town Planning authority.

## 📐 Architectural 3D Spatial Envelope Visualizer

The platform features an interactive 3D spatial standards viewer powered by **Three.js** with an accessible **SVG isometric fallback**:

- **Rule 14(3) Setback Model:** Interactive 3D plot displaying the 6.0m highway carriage-way clearance, 3.0m agricultural boundary offset, and permissible building footprint.
- **Rule 44 FAR Massing Envelope:** Multi-tier architectural volume visualizing baseline 1.50 FAR floors and a translucent golden compounding envelope (+0.25 FAR).
- **Rule 52(1)(a) Water Buffer:** 30.0m green buffer zone extending from the Highest Flood Level (HFL) of natural lakes/rivers where construction is strictly prohibited.
- **Section 27(b) Canal Offset:** 4.5m safety offset along irrigation canal channels.
- **Accessibility & Controls:** Interactive orbit/pitch/zoom, auto-orbit pause/play, layer toggles (clearances, massing, dimensions), reduced-motion detection (`prefers-reduced-motion`), and WebGL capability fallback.
- **Civic Disclaimer:** Explicitly labeled as an educational spatial representation, not an engineering construction drawing.

## 🎯 Hackathon Demonstration Walkthrough Guide

Follow these steps to demonstrate the application to hackathon judges:

1. **Dashboard & 3D Spatial Visuals (`/`):**
   - Open the homepage to see the civic-tech hero, KPI statistics, and the **Interactive 3D Architectural Spatial Envelope Visualizer**.
   - Rotate the 3D model, toggle layers (Clearances, Building Envelope, Dimensions), and switch between **Rule 14(3) Setbacks**, **Rule 44 FAR**, and **Rule 52 Water Buffer**.
   - Toggle to **2D Plan View** to showcase the accessible SVG isometric fallback.
2. **Natural-Language Regulatory Q&A (`/qa`):**
   - Click a suggested question chip, e.g., *"What is the minimum setback required from an agricultural land boundary?"*
   - Review the verified answer card: Notice the `VERIFIED CLAUSE` badge, cited rule pill (`Rule 14(3)`), statutory explanation, preserved numerical chips (`3.0m`, `6.0m`), and expandable source evidence panel with direct link to **PDF Page 47**.
3. **Bilingual Hindi Querying:**
   - Switch language to **HI** using the navbar toggle button.
   - Enter a Hindi inquiry: *"सरकार द्वारा भूमि अधिग्रहण पर मुझे कितना मुआवजा मिलेगा?"*
   - Observe the authentic retrieved response citing **धारा 26 से 30 (RFCTLARR Act)** with **100% सांत्वना राशि (Solatium)** and **Page 18** citation.
4. **Honest Insufficient-Evidence Handling:**
   - Ask an unrelated question: *"Can I build an astronomical space telescope launchpad in Bhopal?"*
   - Observe the honest **Insufficient Regulatory Evidence** warning card directing the citizen to the local SDO rather than hallucinating legal advice.
5. **Rulebook Explorer & Deep Linking (`/regulations`):**
   - Open **Explore Regulations** to browse all 11 indexed rules.
   - Filter by legislation or category, or search `"FAR"`.
   - Click to inspect **Rule 44 (Table 7)**: Inspect the preserved **Table 7 Schedule Table**, mandatory conditions, and embedded **3D Spatial Envelope Preview**.
6. **Session Query History (`/history`):**
   - View previously asked questions with verification badges, timestamps, and one-click re-query actions.
